# Measure Theory · Interactive

**Lebesgue integration and abstract measure spaces**

**Status:** Planned

Part of [Hengzhe's Science Classroom](https://hengzhe-science-classroom.github.io/hub/) · Tier 5 · Research Frontier

## What You'll Learn

- σ-algebras and measures
- Measurable functions and integration
- Convergence theorems (DCT, MCT, Fatou)
- Lp spaces and duality
- Product measures and Fubini's theorem
- Radon-Nikodym and differentiation

- Gerald B. Folland, *Real Analysis: Modern Techniques and Their Applications* (2nd ed., Wiley)
- Halsey Royden & Patrick Fitzpatrick, *Real Analysis* (4th ed., Pearson)
- Elias M. Stein & Rami Shakarchi, *Real Analysis* (Princeton Lectures in Analysis III)
